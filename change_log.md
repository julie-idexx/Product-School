# change_log.md

*Running log of decisions and what changed, and why.*

| Date | Change | Why |
|------|--------|-----|
| 2026-09-14 | Day 1 of discovery phase for the Comeback experience | Day-7 retention dropped 48% → 39% after the v2 streak/notification redesign; kicking off discovery to find how to bring users back after a broken streak |
| 2026-09-16 | Ran an agentic interview round (P3L2a) against the Comeback screen prototype — Claude in character as Priya, Tom, and Amara. Added a third stat, **lifetime lessons total**, to the completion screen, and softened the sub-copy from "a new streak starts now — and your record is still yours" to "today restarts your streak — but it doesn't restart you." | Tom and Amara both independently said the "Day 1" counter undercut the gentler copy around it — no matter how kind the wording, the big number still read as "starting over." Surfacing lifetime lesson count (data Streakly already has) gives the user a stat that *doesn't* reset, so the screen's "coach not scorekeeper" intent survives contact with the one number that was contradicting it. Held off on the other top finding from this round — all three personas wanted the streak-freeze offered *before* a lapse, not just banked reactively on this screen — because it would reopen the already-approved prospective-only, post-lapse-focused decision; flagging for a follow-up round rather than changing it on one lightweight interview pass. |
