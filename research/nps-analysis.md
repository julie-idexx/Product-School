# NPS Feedback Analysis — Streak & Comeback Experience

**Source:** 10 raw NPS comments (unscored/unsegmented as provided). Small sample — directional, not statistically representative.

## Themes Mentioned More Than Once (Ranked by Frequency)

| Rank | Theme | Mentions | Comments |
|------|-------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Streak reset/loss feels punishing, not motivating | 4 | "Felt pointless to start over"; "punishes me for missing a day"; "make me feel like I failed and have to start from scratch"; "the whole thing lost its meaning" |
| 2 (tie) | No recovery mechanism after breaking a streak | 3 | "There was no way to recover it. Other apps let you freeze a streak"; "make coming back easier... instead of start from scratch"; "felt pointless to start over" |
| 2 (tie) | Breaking the streak causes immediate disengagement/churn | 3 | "Haven't opened the app since"; "Deleted after 3 weeks"; "the second I lost it, I was done" |
| 2 (tie) | Users want a meaningful pull back, not generic reminders | 3 | "If it pulled me back with something useful I'd come back"; "helps me get back on track"; "make coming back easier" |
| 5 (tie) | Notification fatigue (frequency/relevance) | 2 | "started to feel like nagging"; "got three in one afternoon and just turned them all off" |
| 5 (tie) | Desire for a "coach," not a "scorekeeper" | 2 | "coach that helps me get back on track, not a scorekeeper that punishes me"; "make me feel like I failed" |
| 5 (tie) | Praise for lesson content / first-week experience | 2 | "The first week was genuinely fun"; "Love the lessons" |

**Noted but single-mention (below the "more than once" bar):** one comment flagged that the home screen looks identical whether a user is on a 2-day streak or returning after two weeks away — "nothing acknowledges where I am." Only one person said it directly, but it's consistent with (and likely an underlying cause of) the "no meaningful pull back" theme above, so it's included as supporting context in the recommendations below rather than as its own ranked theme.

## Praise vs. Complaints

**Praise (2 comments):**
- "The first week was genuinely fun."
- "Love the lessons."

**Complaints (8 comments):** everything else — concentrated on the punitive nature of the streak reset, the lack of any recovery path, notification fatigue, and the absence of any acknowledgment of where a user actually is when they return.

Note the shape of this: the praise is about the *content* (lessons), and every complaint is about the *mechanics around* the content (streak, notifications, return experience). Nobody criticized what Streakly teaches — only how it treats people once they slip.

## Top 3 Actionable Issues

1. **Give users a way to recover after a broken streak.** This is the highest-frequency theme and the most directly requested fix — multiple users explicitly asked for something like a streak freeze, and named the lack of one as the reason they disengaged or deleted the app. This validates the Comeback experience already in discovery.
2. **Tune notification frequency and relevance.** A cheap, fast fix relative to the others — users are opting out entirely ("turned them all off") after receiving multiple notifications in one afternoon, which forecloses any future re-engagement nudge, however good it is.
3. **Acknowledge the user's actual state on return, and lead with something useful rather than a reset.** Ties together the "coach not scorekeeper" request and the home-screen observation: users don't just want the streak preserved, they want the app to recognize whether they're 2 days in or returning after 2 weeks, and respond accordingly — with something to *do*, not just a blank slate.

## Bottom Line for Marcus

Across 10 comments, the product's content isn't in question — the lessons get praised twice and criticized zero times. Every complaint is about what happens around a broken streak: the reset feels punitive, there's no way back in, and the notifications meant to re-engage people are instead driving them to opt out. This is independent, quantitative-adjacent confirmation of what came out of the user interviews: the Comeback experience is the right thing to be building, and a streak-freeze / graceful-return mechanic is the most requested and most churn-linked fix in this data.
