# strategy.md

## The Drop

Day-7 retention fell 9 points — 48% to 39% — over the last two quarters, right after a v2 redesign of the streak and notification system.

## Working Hypothesis

Users churn because breaking a streak feels like punishment: the counter resets to zero and there's no graceful way back in. Once someone misses two days, roughly 80% never return. Streakly hasn't given lapsed users a reason to come back that feels personal, forgiving, and easy.

## Key Tension

Re-engagement nudges vs. notification fatigue — pulling lapsed users back without adding to the kind of nagging that may have contributed to the drop in the first place (the current "you lost your streak" push has a harsh tone and offers nothing when tapped).

## Candidate Direction (not yet committed)

A Comeback experience: when a streak breaks, instead of a cold reset, show the user their best-streak stat, a 60-second comeback lesson to rebuild momentum, and a one-tap streak freeze to protect what they rebuild. Confirmed technically feasible with existing data sources; still needs logic for targeting (who sees it) and freeze rules.

## Open Decision

How to bring users back after a broken streak. Still in discovery — no designs, no committed scope.
